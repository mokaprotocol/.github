## What we're building

Moka is an onchain trading ecosystem designed to make every Solana asset accessible through spot and perpetual markets.

From newly launched tokens to established assets, Moka aims to bring markets, liquidity, and trading infrastructure together in one place.

Our focus is on building the underlying systems that make this possible: onchain order books, multi-maker liquidity, perpetual markets, and efficient risk management.
